## 4. PHASE 2: THE v2 BUILD (Retention, Economics & Paywall)
*Goal: Turn a 3-day app into a daily habit. Fix the unit economics. Implement the viral loop.*

### Step 4.1: Proactive Check-in Engine (Tackle 3 & Need 1)
**Command to Opus:** "Implement a local intelligence layer using CoreML and BackgroundTasks. The app must generate local push notifications based on time-of-day patterns and previous chat context. Example: 'Hey, your interview was today. How did it go?' Do NOT use cloud APIs for this."
* **Need Alignment:** A reason to open the app tomorrow.

### Step 4.2: Emotional Growth Proof System (Tackle 4 & Need 3)
**Command to Opus:** "Build the 'Weekly Growth Report' view. A beautiful, shareable Instagram-story format card. It must calculate the week's mood delta, identify the most common emotion, and generate a 1-sentence personalized note from the AI. Add a 1-tap share button."
* **Need Alignment:** Proof they are getting better. This is the Viral Loop (K-factor > 1).

### Step 4.3: The 3-Tier Cost Architecture & The Paywall Gap (Tackle 6)
**Command to Opus:** "Implement a message routing system. Tier 1 (On-device CoreML) for simple greetings. Tier 2 (Cached) for standard CBT responses. Tier 3 (Claude Opus API) for deep emotional conversations. Then, build a Value-First Paywall using RevenueCat."
* **The Paywall Strategy:** Do NOT put a hard paywall at launch. Let them experience the Emotional Fingerprint and 7 days of Growth Reports.
* **Trigger:** Soft paywall hits after the 5th deep AI message in a day, or when accessing advanced insights. $8.99/mo.

### Step 4.4: Visible Crisis Protocol (Tackle 8)
**Command to Opus:** "Build a 'Safety First' mode. If the AI detects crisis language, it must stay present for 2 exchanges, then trigger a beautiful full-screen safe space UI with 988 and text lines. Do not send this data to analytics."

## 7. THE APPLE-LEVEL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
*To be enforced in every single Opus prompt.*

*   **Fully Responsive Architecture:** Fluidly adapts to any screen size, specifically optimized for all iOS devices (iPhone, iPad).
*   **Zero unnecessary animation.** If it doesn't communicate state, remove it.
*   **Every tap has a haptic.** No touch is silent.
*   **Every destructive action warns first.** No data loss without confirmation.
*   **No AI slop.** Every AI response feels human, warm, specific.
*   **No emojis in the UI.** SF Symbols only. Ever.
*   **Typography:** SF Pro Display for Hero/Titles. SF Pro Text for Body.
*   **Colors:** Base `#0F0F12`. Primary CTA `#FF2D78`. Insights `#00E5CC`.

> **FINAL FOUNDER CHECK:** Does the user feel heard in minute 3? Are we building a bridge to the real world, or a digital cage? If it's a bridge, ship it.
